Version: 2.1.1
Date: 2026-10-23
  Features:
    - Presets: Relaxed, Standard (for experienced players, equals the old defaults), Hardcore, or Custom for the individual settings.
    - Lab speed now follows the debt: +20% at zero, +10% below 0.5, +5% below 1.0, -10% above 3.0 and -20% above 5.0 (Standard).
    - Every Knowledge Consolidation level adds a permanent 1% lab productivity, as a technology effect shown in the research screen.
  Balancing:
    - Knowledge Consolidation comes in four tiers following the science packs (I red+green 8 levels, II +blue 10, III +purple/yellow 12, IV +space infinite); only the highest unlocked tier is active. Each level removes 10/15/20/25% of the current debt (at least 0.25) instead of a fixed 0.25, costs base x 1.2^(L-1) units at 45 s, and a reversed level gives back exactly what it removed. Levels of the old single Knowledge Consolidation technology are not carried over.
  Gui:
    - The window shows the lab speed curve with your current step, the next milestone and the active preset.
  Bugfixes:
    - Knowledge Consolidation only asked for red science on Factorio 2.1 (science packs are items there, not tools).
  Changes:
    - The debt-free bonus setting, step, tier scaling, consolidation value, request interval and maximum multiplier are ignored unless the preset is Custom.

Version: 2.1.0
Date: 2026-10-21
  Features:
    - Every non-infinite technology adds debt; the global technology price multiplier follows it (linear or exponential).
    - Infinite technologies (from any mod) ignore the multiplier and never add debt.
    - Knowledge Consolidation: infinite research that removes debt, with escalating cost and immune to the multiplier.
    - Archive: a chest that consumes valuable items (quality-aware, diminishing returns) to pay off debt.
    - Archive requests: every 15 minutes the Archive asks for a craftable item at 3x value; the next three requests are shown in advance.
    - Trades: slow down labs, raise enemy evolution, or forget a technology in exchange for debt.
    - Research Debt window (Ctrl+K / shortcut bar / HUD button) explaining the multiplier, its breakdown and every way to lower it.
    - Commands /rd-status, /rd-recalc, /rd-reset, /rd-gui and a remote interface "research-debt".
    - Adding the mod to an existing save recalculates debt from the technologies already researched.
  Balancing:
    - Debt per technology scales with its science tier (red x0.5 ... promethium x4), startup setting rd-tier-scaling.
    - Debt-free bonus: +20% lab speed for every force while the debt is zero (map setting rd-debt-free-bonus).
    - Default debt per technology is 0.1 (+10% research cost per technology in linear mode).
  Locale:
    - English and Dutch.
  Info:
    - Version scheme: 2.0.x for Factorio 2.0, 2.1.x for Factorio 2.1, same features.
